Two reasons to move this year

The window for action is unusually open right now.

State retirement mandates

State retirement mandates are spreading fast.

Eighteen states (and counting) have now enacted laws requiring employers to either offer a qualified retirement plan or enroll workers in a state-run auto-IRA. Massachusetts joined the list in the FY2026 budget with its Secure Choice Savings Program; once implementation begins, employers with 25 or more employees without a plan will be subject to the mandate.

A 401(k) gives you and your employees significantly higher contribution limits than a state auto-IRA, plus the design flexibility a state program can't match. If you're going to be required to offer something, it's worth offering something better.

Federal tax incentives

The federal government is paying you to start a plan.

The SECURE Act 2.0 made small-business retirement plans dramatically more affordable. Eligible employers with 50 or fewer employees may qualify for tax credits covering up to 100% of plan startup costs for the first three years, plus additional credits for employer contributions and auto-enrollment features.

Combined, these credits may total up to $16,500+ over the first three years. For many businesses, that means the IRS effectively underwrites the plan during the period when cost matters most.

SECURE 2.0 tax credits

The credits that change the math.

For eligible small employers, three federal tax credits stack on top of one another. Eligibility depends on headcount, plan design, and employee compensation. We'll help you confirm which apply to your situation.

The credit Up to What it covers
Plan Startup Credit $5,000 / year for 3 years 100% of qualified plan setup and administration costs (employers with ≤50 employees; reduced for 51–100).
Employer Contribution Credit $1,000 per employee, per year A credit on the employer match or non-elective contributions for employees earning ≤$100,000. Phases down years 3–5.
Auto-Enrollment Credit $500 / year for 3 years Adding an eligible automatic enrollment feature to a new or existing plan.

Combined

Up to $16,500+ in tax credits over the first three years.

Plan Design & Setup

We design the plan around your business — Safe Harbor, traditional 401(k), Roth, profit-sharing, or owner-only Solo 401(k). We benchmark your options against your goals (retention, tax efficiency, owner contribution maximization) and coordinate setup end-to-end with the recordkeeper and TPA.

Open-architecture, fee-only investment lineup. We select and monitor funds based on cost, performance, and suitability — without revenue-sharing arrangements that distort our recommendations. We meet with you to review the lineup at least annually and document the rationale.

A plan only works if employees use it. We run enrollment meetings, plain-language Q&A sessions, and one-on-one consultations. Higher participation means a healthier plan, easier compliance testing, and a benefit your team actually values.
